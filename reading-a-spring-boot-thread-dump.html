Reading a Spring Boot thread dump without panic

2026-10-305 min read

A thread dump looks like noise until you know what to look for. This is the order I read one in.

Context

When a service stops responding, a thread dump is the quickest snapshot of what every thread is doing right now.

Take two or three dumps a few seconds apart. One dump shows a moment; several show what is stuck.

Find the blocked threads

Start with threads in the BLOCKED or WAITING state and group them by the stack frame they share.

What the patterns mean

  • Many threads waiting on the same lock point to contention.
  • Many threads inside the same database call point to a slow query or an exhausted connection pool.

Next steps

Name the shared resource, then fix or limit it. Take another dump after the change to confirm.
